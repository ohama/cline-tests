Restart attempt history (context for plan 02-03 / Phase 5):
  Two earlier live restart attempts (evidence at
  phase-02/results/20260829T184656Z-inf01/) failed identically at the
  bootstrap step with "Bootstrap failed: 5: Input/output error". Root cause:
  launchctl bootout is asynchronous — it returns once unload is requested,
  not once the job is actually torn down. flashnext holds a 104 GiB model, so
  teardown takes a few seconds; bootstrapping into a still-registered label
  during that window produces the opaque I/O error. Fixed in
  phase-02/infra/restart_service.sh (commit 0ca2645) by polling after bootout
  until the label is no longer found by `launchctl print` AND the port has no
  listener, plus a 3s settle margin, before bootstrapping. This restart
  (attempt 3, first with the fix) succeeded on the first try: teardown
  confirmed after 2s.
