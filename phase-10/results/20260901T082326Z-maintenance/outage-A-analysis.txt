Restart A outage measurement (from outage-A.tsv, 0.5s sampler)
================================================================

KNOWN LIMITATION (discovered during this task, fixed before Restart B):
the ad hoc sampler used `$(( $(date +%s) * 1000 ))` for epoch_ms -- macOS's
BSD `date` has no sub-second format specifier, so this is actually 1-second
resolution masquerading as milliseconds, not true millisecond resolution.
Two samples 0.5s apart can and did land in the same integer second. This
does not change PASS/FAIL classification (still correctly distinguishes 200
from non-200), only the precision of the outage duration figure below.
Restart B's sampler uses python3's time.time() for genuine millisecond
resolution.
