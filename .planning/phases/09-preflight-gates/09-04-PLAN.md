🔴 이 플랜의 결말은 **셋**이다 — `Phase 10 진행` · `마일스톤 종료` · `판정 보류 — 재측정 필요`.

Adjudicate Phase 9: compare this phase's reproduced results against `09-RESEARCH.md`, decide both
gates on the **reproduced** values, and issue the written verdict that ROADMAP Phase 9 success
criterion 5 requires — "Phase 10 진행" or "마일스톤 종료".

Purpose: plans 09-01 through 09-03 deliberately measured without judging. This plan judges. It is
the only place in Phase 9 where a gate verdict may be declared, and the verdict must dispatch on
what this phase measured, not on what the research reported. The research is the *comparand*, not
the authority: agreement between fresh and researched values is the pass condition, and
disagreement is a finding to investigate and publish, not a failure to hide.

Output: `phase-09/GATE-VERDICT.md` — the document Phase 10 reads to know whether it may start, and
the document the milestone reads if it stops here instead.

**Outcome neutrality:** stopping the milestone is a legitimate shipping outcome for this project
(PROJECT.md: "게이트에서 멈추면 그것이 출하물이다"). This plan's success criteria are satisfied by a
well-evidenced "마일스톤 종료" exactly as much as by "Phase 10 진행". What would fail this plan is a
verdict that is unsupported, hedged into ambiguity, or copied from the research.

  the **measured token cost**, not on whether Cline attaches the field — because Phase 9 confirmed
  that Cline *does* attach it (see `PRB-04-FINDINGS.md` §3) and the gate passes anyway only if the
  measured cost is nil. Weigh both halves of the evidence and say which governs: the **controlled**
  replay comparison in 09-03 (identical messages, field present vs absent) is decisive; 09-02's
  multi-turn growth is corroborating and is confounded by reply-length differences. If the
  real-trace deltas differ from the synthetic ones, the real-trace result governs. If the
  controlled and multi-turn evidence point in opposite directions, that conflict is itself a
  finding and must be written up in §3 rather than averaged away.

**Anti-flake / INDETERMINATE rule — state it in the document, then apply it.** A gate may be
decided only on values that `record_verdict` marked `CONFIRMED` or `CONFIRMED-NEGATIVE`. A
`PROVISIONAL-NEGATIVE`, a `DISCARDED-FLAKE`, or an `INDETERMINATE` sample **may not** decide a
gate. If a gate's evidence is INDETERMINATE, the verdict is neither "진행" nor "종료" on that gate's
account: record the gate as **UNDECIDED**, state precisely what re-measurement would settle it,
and route the verdict to the checkpoint in Task 3 as a blocked decision. A single flaky run must
never flip a milestone.

**§5 — Verdict.** Exactly one of these two literal strings, as its own heading, per ROADMAP Phase 9
success criterion 5:
- `Phase 10 진행` — only if both PRB-01 and PRB-04 are decided and both are positive.
- `마일스톤 종료` — if either gate is decided negative.

`판정 보류 — 재측정 필요` heading with the re-measurement plan, and let the Task 3 checkpoint decide.)
