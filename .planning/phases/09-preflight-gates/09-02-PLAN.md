<objective>
Re-measure the `prompt_tokens` oracle from scratch, then use that same oracle to run the two
multi-turn sequences ROADMAP Phase 9 success criterion 4 requires — thinking on vs thinking off,
at least 3 turns each — and capture the real reasoning traces that plan 09-03 needs.

Purpose: two things in this phase are read from the same instrument, the `Prefill started`
`prompt_tokens` line in `flashnext.err`, so they belong together.

First, Phase 10's entire reach proof (VRF-01/VRF-02) rests on "the same user message produces a
different `prompt_tokens` under a different alias". `09-RESEARCH.md` found something important
here: the **absolute** baselines have drifted from `VALIDATED.md` (13/11/41/53 today vs
23/21/51/63 originally) while the **deltas** are bit-for-bit identical (medium −2, low +28,
xhigh +40). The oracle must therefore be used as a delta, never as an absolute — and `medium`'s
−2 margin is too tight to serve as a reach probe. This plan verifies that independently rather
than inheriting it.

Second, the research answered PRB-04 with a single-shot **replay** probe. That probe is decisive
about token cost, but it is not the multi-turn growth comparison the roadmap asks for. Running two
real ≥3-turn sequences — one at `xhigh` feeding each turn's `reasoning` back in the way Cline's own
code does, one with thinking off — measures context growth the way it would actually accumulate,
and produces genuine long `xhigh` traces as a by-product, closing `09-RESEARCH.md` open item #1
(the zero-cost result was obtained with a ~1,380-char **synthetic** trace).

Output: `phase-09/probe_prb03_oracle.sh`, `phase-09/probe_multiturn_growth.py`, a run directory
holding the raw sweep, the multi-turn transcripts and the captured real traces, and
`phase-09/PRB-03-ORACLE.md`.

**Outcome neutrality:** this plan does not require the deltas to match the research, and does not
require the thinking-on sequence to grow no faster than thinking-off. It requires fresh,
per-request-attributed measurements and an honest comparison. Divergence from the research, or
faster growth under thinking, is a finding to record and hand to 09-04 — not a failure. PRB-03 is
not a gate; the PRB-04 gate is adjudicated in 09-04, not here.
</objective>

   `grep -oE 'prompt_tokens=[0-9]+' | cut -d= -f2`
5. Append to `"$run_dir"/prb03.tsv`: sweep, effort, http_code, prompt_tokens, matched log line.
