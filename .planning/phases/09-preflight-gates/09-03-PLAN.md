<objective>
Independently reproduce the controlled PRB-04 measurement — the 🔴 kill-condition gate — and record
the source-inspection correction that came out of it.

Purpose: PRB-04 asks whether a thinking trace returns into the next turn's context and costs
tokens. If it does, it stacks on top of v1's confirmed defect ("compaction does not prune under
real agent load"), the 32K ceiling arrives sooner, and the milestone is discarded. `09-RESEARCH.md`
measured a `prompt_tokens` delta of **zero** at both layers and concluded the gate passes — but on
a single run with a single synthetic ~1,380-char trace. A milestone-killing gate cannot rest on
that. This plan re-runs the probe from the research's literal code, and re-runs it again with the
**real** `xhigh` traces plan 09-02 captured — the insurance the research itself flagged as missing.

This is the *controlled* half of the PRB-04 evidence: identical messages, reasoning field present
vs absent, so the delta is attributable to the field alone. Plan 09-02's multi-turn sequences are
the corroborating half, and are confounded by reply-length differences. 09-04 weighs both.

The source claim matters independently: the research found that
`docs/plan-act-reasoning-implementation.md` §T2's conclusion — that source inspection points toward
"reasoning does NOT come back into context" — is a **false negative**, produced by inspecting only
`toGatewayRequestMessages` (`compat.ts`) and `agentic-compaction.ts`'s `reasoningChars`
self-telemetry. The real path is `shouldIncludeReasoningHistory` (`ai-sdk.ts`) plus
`agentPartToContentBlock` (`agent-message-codec.ts`): Cline **does** attach reasoning history by
default for non-Cerebras providers. The gate still passes, because the model-layer measurement
shows the replay is free — but the project's documents are wrong and must be corrected. Phase 12
owns the edits; **Phase 9 must record the finding so it cannot be lost.**

Output: two probe scripts, one source-verification script, a run directory with fresh evidence,
and `phase-09/PRB-04-FINDINGS.md`.

**Outcome neutrality:** this plan does not require the delta to be zero. It requires a fresh,
flake-discriminated measurement at both layers with both a synthetic and a real trace. A non-zero
delta is a finding — arguably the most important one this milestone could produce — and recording
it with evidence is a **passing** outcome for this plan. The gate verdict itself is 09-04's.
</objective>
