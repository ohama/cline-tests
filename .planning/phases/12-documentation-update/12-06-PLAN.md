<objective>
Two v1-era documents assert a fixed wire `max_tokens = 2048`, and one of them builds overshoot
arithmetic on it. Phase 11 measured 20983 at the now-installed cline, and then measured something
more important: **it is not fixed at all.** Across 278 server-log samples, cline sizes the completion
budget per request so `prompt_tokens + max_tokens` lands near `contextWindow × 0.9` (≈26,100), with
the sum ranging 9,442–32,013 and **zero** breaches of the server's 32,768 limit.

The "+ max_tokens" term in `32k-compaction-policy.md` §4's inequality is therefore no longer a small,
roughly-constant addend. This is outside USE-04/USE-05's named files and is in scope by explicit
decision (`phase-12/SCOPE-DECISIONS.md` item 1): leaving a known-false number on disk is what this
phase exists to prevent.

Purpose: eliminate the last known-false numeric claim in `docs/`.
Output: two corrected documents plus one annotated formula.
</objective>
