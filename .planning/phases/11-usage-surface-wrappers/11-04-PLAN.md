<objective>
Answer the three open research items that only a live probe can answer, and build the safety
envelope that lets Phase 11 issue live requests without re-using a rule Phase 10 has already
corrected.

Purpose: `11-RESEARCH.md` closes with four items it could not settle statically. Three of them are
resolved here: (Q4) what a leaked `--thinking high` actually does through a `hosted_vllm/` alias;
(Q6.2) whether Cline's fixed per-turn `max_tokens` is still 2048 at 3.0.60; (Q5) whether repeated
`-m` invocations — which the wrappers now generate constantly — can ever move `model` or
`contextWindow`, not just `updatedAt`. The fourth (A/B sizing) belongs to plan 11-05.

The envelope matters as much as the answers. `phase-10/probe_lib10.sh`'s `postflight10` hard-fails
on **any** `providers.json` sha256 change — and `cline -m` rewrites `updatedAt` on every single
call (`phase-10/PHASE-10-FINDINGS.md` §4.4). Re-using it here would make every wrapper invocation
report a violation. The project constraint was corrected to `model` and `contextWindow`
(`.planning/STATE.md`); this plan is the first to encode that correction in an instrument, and must
show the instrument still discriminates a real change rather than having simply been loosened.

Output: `phase-11/probe_lib11.sh` + its self-test, two probe scripts, and `phase-11/OPEN-ITEMS.md`.

**Outcome neutrality:** every measurement here is recorded as observed. `--thinking high` may return
500, 400, 200, or something nobody predicted; `max_tokens` may be 2048 or may have moved; the
repeated-`-m` probe may find drift or may find none. Each of those is a complete, satisfying answer.
The only failure mode this plan has is issuing requests and not recording what came back.

**Live-request budget, stated up front and enforced in code: 14.** Planned: 4 for open item 1
(2 curl pairs), 1 for open item 2, 6 wrapper invocations for open item 3, 3 spare for a single
operational retry. `probe_lib11.sh` maintains a counter file and **aborts** when the cap is reached
rather than trusting anyone to remember it — plan 10-04 fired 48 against a stated 17 and had to
disclose it (`phase-10/PHASE-10-FINDINGS.md` §4.5). The counter is on **model requests observed in
the server log**, not only on invocations, because one cline invocation can be several turns.
</objective>
