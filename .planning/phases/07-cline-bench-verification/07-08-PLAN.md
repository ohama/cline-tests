<objective>
Show the user what a cline-bench task now actually costs on this stack, and let them decide how
many more to run.

Purpose: the 07-03 checkpoint's cost picture was built on a run that spent 5.3s failing to reach
the model — ~86% of its 232s was fixed setup. A task that really runs the agent loop has a
completely different cost shape, and the user's earlier `stop-at-one` reasoning ("more runs buy
repeated evidence of one known limitation") was specific to a broken injection. They deserve the
new numbers before being asked again, and they deserve to be asked rather than assumed.

Output: `cost.md` (what the user was shown, with sources), `decision2.md` (their answer, verbatim),
and `phase-07/bench/SELECTED_TASKS_GAP` (the machine-readable consequence).

**This plan runs no bench tasks.** It measures, asks, and records.
</objective>
