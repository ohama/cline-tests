<objective>
Bring the machine's own record of itself back into agreement with the machine, and then account for
the whole phase requirement by requirement.

Purpose: `~/local-llm-settings/` is the house's living reference — its own header says *"이 디렉터리는
살아 있는 기준점이다. 시점 스냅샷(백업)이 아니다."* Its `config/litellm-config.yaml` is generated
output, copied one-directionally **from** `/Users/ohama/agent-stack/litellm/config.yaml` by
`sync.sh`. Plan 10-03 changed the real file and deliberately left the mirror alone; until `sync.sh`
runs, the house's reference is describing a machine that no longer exists. CFG-15 and ROADMAP
criterion 5 both ask for exactly this, and criterion 5 carries a 2026-09-01 correction saying so in
as many words: fix the original, then refresh the copy — never the other way round.

Then write the phase's account of itself. Phase 10 is the first phase in this milestone that
mutated the stack; the record of what was changed, what was proven, and what was proven only weakly
is the thing Phases 11 and 12 build on.

Output: a synced mirror with its own commit, an updated `phase-10/BASELINE.txt`, and
`phase-10/PHASE-10-FINDINGS.md`.

**Outcome neutrality:** the findings document reports the phase as it happened. If CFG-16 came back
negative, if the window was rolled back, if VRF-04 saw no reasoning — those are recorded as results
with their evidence, and the document is complete when every requirement has an honest disposition,
not when every requirement is green.
</objective>

**Step 1 — pre-state.** Record sha256 of `/Users/ohama/agent-stack/litellm/config.yaml` and of
`~/local-llm-settings/config/litellm-config.yaml`, and `diff` them into `"$RUN"/pre-diff.txt`. They

back). Record which.

**Step 2 — dry run first.** `cd ~/local-llm-settings && ./sync.sh --check` into
`"$RUN"/sync-check-before.txt`, recording its exit code. Running the read-only check before the
write shows what `sync.sh` believes is out of date, so its subsequent effect is a confirmation
rather than a surprise.

**Step 3 — sync.** `cd ~/local-llm-settings && ./sync.sh` into `"$RUN"/sync-run.txt`. This copies
the real config into the mirror, regenerates `STATE.md` — whose `## litellm 별칭` table is parsed
live from `/Users/ohama/agent-stack/litellm/config.yaml` by an embedded `python3`/`yaml.safe_load`
snippet, so the new aliases appear there without anyone typing them — and regenerates `SHA256SUMS`
over the tree.

**Step 4 — the acceptance check (ROADMAP criterion 5, literally).**
`cd ~/local-llm-settings && ./sync.sh --check` into `"$RUN"/sync-check-after.txt`. It must report
agreement with the real system (`✅ 실제 시스템과 일치한다`) and exit 0. This dry-run output is the
evidence for *"sync.sh 실행 결과에 이번 변경이 반영된다"*; capture it verbatim.

**Step 5 — independent confirmations**, not relying on `sync.sh`'s own self-report:
- `diff /Users/ohama/agent-stack/litellm/config.yaml ~/local-llm-settings/config/litellm-config.yaml`
  must now be empty, and their sha256s must match.
- `grep -n 'flashnext-plan\|flashnext-act\|flashnext-reach-xhigh' ~/local-llm-settings/STATE.md` —
  record what is found. If plan 10-03 installed the aliases, all three should appear in the
  regenerated alias table; if it rolled back, none should, and that agreement with reality is itself
  the correct result. Record which case holds rather than asserting one.
- `grep -c 'litellm-config.yaml' ~/local-llm-settings/SHA256SUMS` ≥ 1 and the recorded hash matches
  the file.

**Step 6 — commit the mirror's own repository.** `~/local-llm-settings` **is** a git repo, while
`/Users/ohama/agent-stack/litellm/` is **not** (`git rev-parse --show-toplevel` fails there) — so
this commit is the only version history the change will ever have outside `phase-10/backups/`.
`git -C ~/local-llm-settings add -A` and commit with a message in the repo's existing style,
naming the three aliases and the reason (Plan/Act reasoning injection, `hosted_vllm/` prefix). Do
**not** push. Record the resulting commit hash and `git -C ~/local-llm-settings status --porcelain`
(expected empty) into `"$RUN"/mirror-commit.txt`.

**Step 7 — re-baseline.** Append a clearly-labelled `## post-sync (Phase 10 plan 06)` section to
`phase-10/BASELINE.txt` with the new sha256 of the mirror, of the real config, and of
`providers.json` (unchanged, and assert it). **Append — do not overwrite** the pre-mutation section:
the original values are what `CFG-13-EVIDENCE.md` and `rollback_config.sh` verify against, and
destroying them would break the rollback path that is still the standing recovery procedure.
