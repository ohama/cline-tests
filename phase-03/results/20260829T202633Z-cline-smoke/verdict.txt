--- Classification ---

Per the plan's explicit catch-all: "(C) ... also the catch-all: ... Otherwise (C) means the
failure demands a punch-through broader than a specific Cline-owned directory ... or the process
died on a signal with no diagnostic ... Make no profile change." A generic, path-less runtime
error that cannot be attributed to any single pre-declared candidate directory is exactly the kind
of ambiguous result the plan instructs to treat as (C) rather than guess at a bounded fix. Per
Rule 4 (deviation-classifier) and the plan's own "if unsure, widening needs a human" instruction:
NO change was made to phase-03/sandbox/config.env's EXTRA_ALLOW_PATHS or to any other sandbox
artifact. EXTRA_ALLOW_PATHS remains empty (default), confirmed by
`grep -n EXTRA_ALLOW_PATHS phase-03/sandbox/config.env` after this run.

--- Why this does not fail the phase ---

None of the four ROADMAP Phase 3 success criteria (SBX-01..04) require the real `cline` binary to
run under the sandbox -- criteria 2 and 3 are already proven at the kernel level by plan 03-03
using `/bin/cat`, `/bin/sh`, and plain `node` (which share cline's fs/exec/subprocess syscall
surface), and Phase 4 owns the actual headless `cline` wrapper this sandbox will run in production.
This is a documented open item handed to Phase 4, not a hidden failure: Phase 4 should expect that
running the real `cline` binary under this sandbox as-is currently fails with a generic Bun
runtime error of unknown specific cause, and should budget time to reproduce with elevated
diagnostic tooling (e.g. `dtruss`/`fs_usage` under sudo, or Bun's own verbose/debug flags if any
exist) before deciding whether -- and how narrowly -- to widen EXTRA_ALLOW_PATHS.
